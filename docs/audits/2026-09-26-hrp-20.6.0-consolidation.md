## Why

With section loading, a strong model loaded nearly every HRP and Universal
section, about 570,000 characters. In the first blinded comparison the answer
written under the protocol beat the one written without it on substance (red
flags, option coverage, concrete next steps) but was faulted for length and
research-process clutter. The server now enforces part of the completion gate
(`finalize_research` verifies signed research receipts), so the prose that
restated those checks can go.

## What changed

No clinical or scientific method content changed. Seven process, output and
meta sections were rewritten around the rules they actually add; everything
else is a pointer to the rule's home section or is now enforced by the server.

## Owner decisions

A. Applied output defaults (say if you want any reverted):

1. No fixed "Analyzed with Heterodox Research Protocol" opener, and no fixed
   preflight label. One line at the end names the protocol versions used and
   AskRigor.com, as Universal requires.
2. Research-process status, `finalize_research` limits and continuation handoffs
   go in one short plain-language limits note near the end; uncertainty is
   stated at the claim it affects.
3. Ordinary answers use a compact comparison table with the columns the decision
   needs; the full 29-column evidence table belongs in a technical audit or debug
   export (original text in the OutputFormatting coverage table).

B. Further cuts proposed, not applied (each needs your yes):

1. Serve the ChatGPT-mode guidance (reasoning tiers, Deep Research pros and cons,
   source-count planning bands) only on ChatGPT surfaces: about 2,300 characters
   elsewhere.
2. Collapse NoSilentOverride to one sentence: about 570 characters; it would also
   cover checks the current list does not name.
3. Show the copyable "check forums for..." prompt only when forum evidence is
   incomplete or inaccessible.
4. Move the RO4 specification field list into ResearchAuditTemplates: about
   1,600 characters.

C. Still open from earlier: the euphemism rule stays as is unless you decide
otherwise.
