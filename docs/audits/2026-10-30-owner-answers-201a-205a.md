# Owner answers 201a and 205a, built for #387 (30 October 2026)

The owner answered "201a 202a 203a 204a 205a" on 30 October 2026, at about 14:45 UTC. This record covers the two
answers that change the build; 204a releases #387 with them, and 202a and 203a change no code.

## 201a: the YouTube scout's group-word check is gone

- **Question:** before the scout sends a research target to Gemini, AskRigor refused a target with no word from a
  fixed English list naming a group of people (people, adults, patients, women and the like). That check refused 6 of
  the 15 runs that called the scout on 27 and 28 October, for example two forms of copper "in humans". It judged a
  meaning with a word list, which AGENTS.md says not to repair by adding words, and it read only English.
- **Answer (a):** drop the group-word check and keep the others.
- **Built:** `researchTargetScreenFailures` (`apps/research-mcp/src/actions/gemini-scout-route.ts`) no longer reports
  `no_group_word`, and that part is gone from `RESEARCH_TARGET_SCREEN_PARTS`. The de-identification screen (first-person
  words, contact details, keys, links, chat roles, record fields, instructions) and the person markers (he or she, a
  title, one person's age, example markers, capitalized pairs, a capitalized word before a narrative verb) are
  unchanged, and the tool still asks for "a group of people and their goal". Targets such as "Two forms of copper in
  humans" and "A runner with knee pain after a marathon" now pass.
- **One consequence:** the English word list also refused every target written in another language. The tool asks for
  an English target, but one in another language now passes when nothing in it matches the markers, most of which read
  English. The privacy data map says so (`docs/privacy-data-map.md`, the scout tool's paragraph); the public privacy
  page describes the screen only as rejecting non-de-identified targets, which stays true of what it checks.
- **Tests:** `tests/scout-population-target.test.ts` (the question's examples pass; each refused target names only the
  parts it still fails), `tests/mcp-gemini-scout-continuation.test.ts` (the refusal's guidance, with targets each
  failing a remaining part), `tests/gemini-scout-action.test.ts` and `tests/gemini-scout-settlement.test.ts` (a
  refused target now names a person).

## 205a: the answer states the limits that matter, and the panel shows them all

- **Question:** owner answer 195a had the AI state every material limit in its own words, and the panel lists every
  limit beneath a checked answer. In the run on #384 the check gave 10 limits for "Is a 20-minute nap in the early
  afternoon okay?" (`q135-low-stakes`), mostly about the research itself, and answers pasted such lists word for word,
  often after the check, which also moved the delivered answer away from the checked one.
- **Answer (a):** the answer states only the limits that change what this person should believe or do, the panel still
  shows them all, the result says so beside the list, and which limits matter stays the AI's judgment.
- **Built, in the result:** beneath a passing check (`ready` or `ready_with_limits`, the statuses under which the panel
  lists limits), the result gives one line, `PASSING_LIMITS_GUIDANCE` in `apps/research-mcp/src/final-check-messages.ts`,
  then each limit as `Limit: ...`. The line says the panel lists every limit, the answer states only those that change
  what this person should believe or do, and a limit that does but is missing from the checked draft is added and the
  draft checked again (the answer is still given word for word as checked). Under a check that has not passed, the
  panel lists no limits, so each limit keeps its `Limit to include in your own words:` line.
- **Built, around it:** the runtime description of `finalize_research` (`FINAL_CHECK_RUNTIME_DESCRIPTION`), the
  runtime descriptions of `answer_draft` and `limits`, and the check line after a passing check with limits, which
  read "with the limits above" and now read "Checked with AskRigor's final check, with the limits it found.", since
  the answer may now state only some of them. All are runtime text; the frozen tool lists are unchanged (owner answer
  189a).
- **HRP 20.6.34** (`protocols/HRP_Full.xml`, SHA-256 `7b933620ecca1116f1d4c45c87046e37d8b2a6f97bc4e7a38b6dfd30aa23c74a`):
  `LimitsNote` said to "include every limit finalize_research returns"; it now says to include each that changes what
  the person should believe or do, and every one when the check has not passed. `FinalizeResearch` says the same on
  `ready_with_limits`. Both rules sit outside the core (`OutputFormatting` and `FinalSelfCheck`), which is byte for byte
  unchanged. The edits are recorded in `tests/fixtures/protocol-edits/2026-10-30-owner-answer-205-hrp.json` and
  reversed by `undoOwnerAnswer205Hrp`, chained under `undoLesson51AndUda444Hrp`, so every older layer still rebuilds its
  bytes; the current-version pins carry 20.6.34. Universal 20.5.46 is unchanged.
- **Tests:** `tests/owner-answer-205-protocol-2026-10-30.test.ts` (both sentences, the result's matching guidance, the
  core, the bytes and the reversal); `tests/finalize-harm-screen.test.ts` (a passing check gives the guidance line and
  `Limit:` lines; a check stopped on the harm screen keeps `Limit to include in your own words`); the check line's
  pins in six test files.
