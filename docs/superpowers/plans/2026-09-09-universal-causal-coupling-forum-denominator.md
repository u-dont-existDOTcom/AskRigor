## Invariants

- A pharmacologic explanation of an adverse marker does not answer whether the
  marker is necessary for, sufficient for, predictive of, or mediating benefit.
- Tolerability-only or mechanism-only evidence cannot establish preserved
  efficacy.
- Every serious live hypothesis gets at least one high-information discriminator
  or a documented inaccessible state.
- A strong formal result does not cancel a separately informative community
  discriminator gap.
- Only a predefined neutral primary corpus can support forum-frequency language.
  Sensitivity/discovery cases remain outside its denominator.
- Search-result estimates never become denominators; only retrieved cards count.
- Partial/unavailable comment retrieval cannot silently enter user prevalence.
- Counts precede qualitative labels; direction, measurement confidence, and
  causal-attribution confidence remain separate.
- Existing frozen studies and the eight captured MAST slots remain unchanged.
