# Executable product-acceptance repair plan

**Goal:** Prevent another broad-treatment answer from passing after skipping the
configured Spark scout, auditing too few or generic videos, pooling materially
different programs, using stale Custom GPT Actions, omitting a video link, or
leaking internal audit language.

## Failure diagnosis

The repeated bad output was not a one-off research miss. Repository checks had
verified instruction fragments and individual tools, but did not verify the
installed product or replay the failed user-facing scenario. Several planning
targets were warnings rather than completion conditions, an Instructions-only
editor update could be called installed while the Action schema stayed stale,
and plugin currency was tracked separately.

## Executable invariants

1. For broad treatment or avoid-surgery research with a substantial YouTube
   corpus, a reconciled Gemini Spark candidate frontier is required. Native
   generic discovery cannot silently substitute for it.
2. When the valid ledger contains at least eight material candidate videos
   across six materially distinct available programs, synthesis is blocked
   until eight videos across six programs have completed transcript and
   discussion auditing. Selected and fully audited counts stay separate.
3. The Custom GPT installation is one reviewed bundle: Instructions, Action
   schema, and Spark skill. Their hashes and the installed operation inventory
   must match; an Instructions-only update is incomplete.
4. A fresh broad-treatment product replay must visibly use the Spark validator,
   transcript Action, and landscape assessor, then pass the structured coverage
   receipt.
5. Ordinary output fails acceptance if it contains internal retrieval codes,
   lock/receipt/sampling jargon, a protocol-compliance preamble, fewer than
   eight linked audited videos under the applicable minimum, or an audited
   entry without a linked title.
6. The personal Codex plugin must be reinstalled from the reviewed source and
   source/installed hashes must match.
