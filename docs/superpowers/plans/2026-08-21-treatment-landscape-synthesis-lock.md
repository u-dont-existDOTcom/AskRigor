# Treatment-landscape synthesis lock

## Objective

Prevent a broad treatment answer from being synthesized after deep auditing of
a narrow or redundant video selection. Keep selection-space adequacy separate
from transcript access and per-video comment-corpus completion.

## Owner requirements

- Inventory materially distinct treatment/program hypotheses before selecting
  videos; the inventory is a discovery taxonomy, not an efficacy claim.
- Do not collapse distinct exercise, rehabilitation, diet, injection,
  procedure, multimodal, nonaction, progression, or eventual-standard-treatment
  trajectories into umbrella labels.
- Treat video and fingerprint counts as planning heuristics, not proof or quotas.
- Make two or three audited videos a hard failure for a broad question when
  additional material treatment classes remain discoverable.
- Compute separate selection-coverage and per-video-depth locks and compose an
  overall treatment-landscape synthesis lock from explicit state.
- Keep the existing transcript Action; do not recreate it.
- Add the two owner-specified hip regressions to canonical HRP.
- Promote the domain-neutral lesson to
  `u-dont-existDOTcom/universal-dev-architecture` with exact AskRigor provenance
  and limits.

## Research-before-reinvention

- Applicability: `required`
- Trigger: repeated refinement of a bespoke research-selection and synthesis
  workflow, including a proposed new Action controller.
- Independent conception: deep source auditing cannot repair a narrow selection
  frame; selection diversity and per-source completeness require independent
  gates.
- Search formulations: treatment/intervention grouping before synthesis;
  complex-intervention dimensions; evidence mapping; relevance-plus-novelty
  selection; state-space/combinatorial coverage; theoretical sampling and gap-
  driven expansion.

### Existing-work map

- Already solved locally: AskRigor option-space classes, exact program
  fingerprints, repeated YouTube batches, transcript verification, resumable
  per-video comment audits, bidirectional formal/community iteration, and
  acquisition-completion locks.
- Partially solved externally: Cochrane intervention grouping and complexity
  guidance separates decision-relevant intervention variants; PRISMA-ScR maps
  broad evidence spaces; maximal marginal relevance composes relevance with
  novelty; NIST combinatorial coverage supplies a state-space coverage analogy.
- Composable: the existing AskRigor survey, transcript, and per-video audit tools
  plus one deterministic coverage evaluator.
- Genuinely unresolved locally: a machine-computed lock consuming uncovered
  treatment classes, redundant fingerprints, unaudited material candidates,
  newly emerging hypotheses, directional searches, and access boundaries.
- Apparently novel: none claimed. The controller is project-specific
  composition, not a validated sampling method or representativeness estimator.

### Decision

- Disposition: `compose`
- Borrowed: intervention grouping, evidence-space mapping,
  relevance/nonredundancy, and explicit state coverage.
- Modified: AskRigor derives classes and fingerprints from the current question
  and sources rather than imposing a fixed disease menu.
- Novel remainder: strict Action schemas and deterministic fail/pass rules that
  fit AskRigor's public, bounded, read-only Action transport.
- Simple baseline: the current instruction-only expected-information-gain rule.
- Intended advantage: causal regressions can prove that known undercoverage
  states block broad synthesis even when every selected video is fully audited.

## Acceptance

- A broad audit of ten near-identical programs blocks synthesis while material
  classes remain uncovered.
- Two or three fully audited videos cannot certify a broad landscape when
  additional material classes are discoverable.
- Renamed fingerprint IDs with identical normalized program fields count once;
  stable channel IDs, not display names, determine source independence.
- Screen counts are derived from reciprocal batch/candidate records; invalid
  records are excluded and reported rather than inflating aggregates.
- New decision-relevant hypotheses, unaudited ranking-relevant candidates,
  missing benefit/no-effect/harm/discontinuation/eventual-treatment searches,
  or `further_expansion_likely_to_improve_answer=yes` block synthesis.
- Numeric planning heuristics produce warnings; state-based omissions produce
  blockers.
- Decision-relevant or uncertain omissions block; a supported
  not-decision-relevant omission warns without creating a select-everything
  quota.
- A genuine access boundary permits only a bounded answer after it is terminal,
  nonretryable, and recovery was attempted; it does not become negative
  evidence or a broad ranking.
- The selected-video record contains title/channel/date/link or ID, exact
  program fingerprint, stage, outcome/horizon, nonredundant contribution,
  transcript boundary, and discussion-audit boundary; missing program fields
  remain `program not described`.
- `get_youtube_transcript` remains Action-only and operational; the new
  coverage assessor is also Action-only; MCP remains exactly 17 tools.
- Canonical HRP version/date/hash and generated packet receipts derive from the
  final exact bytes and all current/historical deployment boundaries remain
  truthful.
