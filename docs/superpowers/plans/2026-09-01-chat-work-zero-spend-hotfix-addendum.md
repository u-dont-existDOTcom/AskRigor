## Scope of supersession

This addendum preserves the parent program’s scientific objective,
Terminal-Bench contribution, benchmark-integrity controls, MAST source pins,
paired-condition principle, and no-tuning-before-sealed-epoch rule.

It supersedes every older instruction or implication that:

- Codex/Work may originate a MAST smoke proposal or cost ceiling;
- the next MAST operational smoke should use paid model API inference;
- an owner-approved nonzero-spend manifest is the only way to execute any MAST
  learning step;
- Codex may choose methodology, scale, or interpret results;
- routine internal ChatGPT supervisor routing requires Joel to relay a prompt,
  paste a packet, or say `send it`;
- a named ChatGPT chat may be credited without an exact source-message receipt.

## Controlling authority

ChatGPT Project Manager/Extra High/Pro owns reasoning, proposals, methodology,
prioritization, spending design, consequential tradeoffs, scientific
interpretation, supervisory verdicts, and selection of the next strategy.

Codex and Work are execution-only for exact source-bound bounded tasks that Chat
cannot execute. They may prepare packets, route exact bytes, capture source
receipts, run deterministic checks, mutate files within the directive, and
return factual evidence. They may not decide what the evidence means or what to
do next.

## Controlling spend decision

Paid model API inference is forbidden. The active model API spend ceiling is
USD 0. The USD 30 smoke proposal and approximately USD 175 pilot ceiling were
unauthorized Codex/Work-authored proposals and have no operative status.

No paid call occurred. No older or hypothetical paid manifest revives the path.
A future nonzero-spend proposal must originate in a verified reasoning chat and
still requires a newer explicit owner decision before execution.

## Controlling routing decision

Routine routing among the Project Manager Chat, specialist ChatGPT supervisor
chats, and Codex/Work is standing owner-authorized internal control-plane
transport.

Codex/Work must route exact factual state automatically. It must never ask Joel
to say `send it`, paste the packet, or act as courier. A generic browser
confirmation rule for third-party representational communication cannot
supersede this narrower internal route.

The exception does not cover publication, external proposal submission,
purchases, messages to third parties, account changes, or other genuinely
external representational actions.

eligible step.

owner-only decision remains, when the authenticated ChatGPT route is unavailable
after the authorized attempt, or when a safety/security/privacy/irreversible
external-action boundary intervenes.
