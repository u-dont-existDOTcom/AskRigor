## Acceptance criteria

- A broad treatment review searches materially different mainstream, contrarian/practitioner, benefit, no-effect/failure, harm/discontinuation, and formal-discriminator hypotheses as applicable; it does not treat provider rank, views, or comment volume as credibility.
- Before audits, a candidate-selection ledger explains each selected video's unique hypothesis, decision usefulness, creator/pool independence, expected firsthand value, and nonredundancy; weak coverage triggers query rewrite, cursor expansion, or another batch.
- Exercise/PT and other umbrella interventions are decomposed into the exact tested or reported program, dose, supervision, fidelity/adherence, co-interventions, stage, and purpose. Preoperative prevention/conservative care is not conflated with postoperative rehabilitation.
- Every decision-driving comparative study states the exact intervention and comparator programs, what the contrast can and cannot establish, and the main transportability constraints. A weak or mismatched comparator narrows inference rather than refuting the whole class.
- Before `support_not_located`, the research distinguishes exact matched outcomes from adjacent human, mechanistic, grey/practitioner, and community support. It reports plausibility, indirectness, safety, cost, reversibility, and opportunity cost separately; it neither dismisses nor inflates the claim.
- The final answer integrates community and formal evidence by intervention and reports what YouTube changed in the evidence map. It cannot claim completion while candidate coverage, cross-layer transfers, or decision-critical comparator audits remain executable.
