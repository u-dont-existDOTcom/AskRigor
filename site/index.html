Read-only research retrieval

Evidence-first retrieval, with the boundary in view.

AskRigor helps connected clients retrieve public research and source metadata while preserving source, access status, coverage, and retrieval context.

AskRigor is a product operated by Mayan Roots.

MCP endpoint for developers

What AskRigor retrieves

Research literature

Structured public metadata from NCBI/PubMed, Europe PMC, and Crossref.

Trial records

Public ClinicalTrials.gov records with provider status and source context.

Public video context

API-visible YouTube video, comment, and reply data with completeness accounting.

What it does not do

AskRigor does not diagnose, does not treat or prescribe, and is not a substitute for professional advice. It returns retrieved information and provenance; it does not make clinical conclusions.

Coverage stays explicit

Access failures and incomplete results are reported as such: provider data may be incomplete, delayed, or unavailable. Retrieved material is not proof of a medical, legal, financial, or scientific conclusion.

Use AskRigor

Use a compatible connected client to make read-only requests. Review the Privacy Notice, Terms, and Support guidance before use.
