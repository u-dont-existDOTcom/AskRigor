Add AskRigor

Add AskRigor to your AI.

AskRigor is an app your AI assistant uses to do the research behind an answer: it searches and reads the studies, checks the figures, and says what it couldn't check. See the difference it makes.

In Claude: works today, free plan included

Claude lets you add an app by its address as a custom connector. The free plan allows one.

  1. In Claude, open your connectors: Settings → Connectors (in some versions, Customize → Connectors).
  2. Choose Add custom connector.
  3. Name it AskRigor and paste this address: https://mcp.askrigor.com/mcp
  4. Sign in to AskRigor when Claude asks.
  5. When Claude asks whether AskRigor may use a tool, choose Always allow, or set it in the connector's settings. One piece of research makes dozens of calls, and asking each time slows it down.

Then ask your question as usual and mention AskRigor, for example: "Use AskRigor: what are the health benefits and toxicity of this plant?" Give it a few minutes. If Claude answers without it, check that AskRigor is switched on in the tools menu under the message box, and ask again. Anthropic's help article explains custom connectors: Getting started with custom connectors.

In ChatGPT: coming to the app directory

AskRigor is coming to ChatGPT's app directory. Once it's there, anyone can add it from the directory, free ChatGPT included, and use it in Instant mode as in our comparison.

Until then, free ChatGPT can't add an app by its address. Some paid ChatGPT plans can, through developer mode in ChatGPT's settings: if yours can, use the same address, https://mcp.askrigor.com/mcp.

Type @AskRigor to use it. Start your message with @AskRigor and pick it from the list that appears, then ask your question. Just naming AskRigor often isn't enough: ChatGPT, in Instant mode especially, may answer on its own without it.

On free ChatGPT, choose Thinking for research questions. It takes longer, and it carries the research further than Instant mode.
