What AskRigor is

AskRigor is an app for AI assistants that does the research behind an answer: it searches and reads the studies, checks the figures, looks at what people report, and says what it couldn't check. AskRigor is a product operated by Mayan Roots LLC.

AskRigor doesn't diagnose, treat or prescribe, and it isn't a substitute for professional advice.
