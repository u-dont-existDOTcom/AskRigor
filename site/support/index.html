Ask for a bigger research job

Most questions fit in one chat with AskRigor in your own AI app. Some jobs are too big for that, such as checking every claim in a long series of videos, a book or a website, or reviewing everything published on one question. For a job like that, ask us for a quote: what the job covers, how it will be done, its price and when it will be ready. Nothing is charged unless you accept it.

Ask for a quote

The button opens an email to joel@askrigor.com with four questions. If no email opens, copy them into an email to that address:

  • What should be researched?
  • Links to what it's about, such as videos, a website or papers
  • When do you need it?
  • Your budget, if you have one

Describe the question in general terms, without health records or anything that identifies you or someone else. This website doesn't store your request: it goes from your email to ours. The quote also says which AI services will read your question and whether anything from the job may be published.

How to contact us

For support, privacy, accessibility, or security questions, email joel@askrigor.com.

What to include

Include the affected route or tool, approximate UTC time, expected behavior, actual behavior, and sanitized error text. This helps us understand the issue without receiving unnecessary personal or sensitive material.

Privacy and accessibility

Tell us about a privacy concern or accessibility barrier, including the page or interaction involved and the assistive technology or browser context if you wish to share it.

Shared questions and answers

If you sent AskRigor a de-identified copy of a question and its answer, ask AskRigor in the same app to delete it, with the code it gave you when you sent it. Without the code, AskRigor cannot tell which copy is yours; every copy is deleted after 90 days anyway.

Security reports

For a suspected security issue, describe the affected route, what you observed, and safe reproduction details. Do not send exploit payloads or secrets.

What not to send

Do not email API keys, passwords, health records, or bulk comment corpora. Please remove credentials and personal data from error text before sending it.

Emergencies and urgent medical concerns

AskRigor is not an emergency service. For an emergency or urgent medical concern, contact local emergency services or a qualified professional.
