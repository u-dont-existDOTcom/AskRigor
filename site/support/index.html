Support

AskRigor is a product operated by Mayan Roots LLC.

How to contact us

For support, privacy, accessibility, or security questions, email joel@askrigor.com.

What to include

Include the affected route or tool, approximate UTC time, expected behavior, actual behavior, and sanitized error text. This helps us understand the issue without receiving unnecessary personal or sensitive material.

Privacy and accessibility

Tell us about a privacy concern or accessibility barrier, including the page or interaction involved and the assistive technology or browser context if you wish to share it.

Security reports

For a suspected security issue, describe the affected route, what you observed, and safe reproduction details. Do not send exploit payloads or secrets.

What not to send

Do not email API keys, passwords, health records, or bulk comment corpora. Please remove credentials and personal data from error text before sending it.

Emergencies and urgent medical concerns

AskRigor is not an emergency service. For an emergency or urgent medical concern, contact local emergency services or a qualified professional.
